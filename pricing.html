CLARITY & CONFIDENCE

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers to common questions about our additive pricing structure, material testing, billing terms, and turnaround.

Pricing is determined primarily by bounding-box volume, raw material density, total machine chamber hours, layer height resolution, and requested post-processing (e.g. vapor smoothing, bead blasting, heat treatment). Our automated DFM quotation engine analyzes your uploaded CAD geometry to calculate exact manufacturing costs with zero manual markup.
We accept STEP (.stp, .step), STL, IGES (.igs), 3MF, OBJ, and native SolidWorks (.sldprt) files. For high-precision dimensional verification and tolerance critical components, we strongly recommend uploading native STEP files to preserve true mathematical NURBS curves.
Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or switch between monthly and annual plans at any point through your client portal. Upgrades are applied immediately with prorated credits for any unused billing duration. Annual subscriptions receive an instant 20% discount across all included builds.
Absolutely. Because additive manufacturing allows automated packing of multiple parts into a single build chamber (especially in SLS and Multi Jet Fusion), per-part production cost drops significantly as quantity increases. Orders exceeding 50 parts receive automated volume discounts of up to 65%.
We provide a comprehensive range of industrial post-processing: standard support removal and bead blasting, automated chemical vapor smoothing (yielding injection-molded smooth, water-sealed finishes), ceramic tumbling, deep black dye baths, thermal stress relief, and custom color painting.
Yes. Every batch processed under our Production and Enterprise OEM tiers can be accompanied by raw material mill test certificates (CoA), optical CMM dimensional inspection scan reports, and certified statements of conformance according to ISO 9001:2015 and AS9100D standards.
