COMMONLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Essential information regarding CAD submission, confidentiality protocols, lead times, and facility access.

Our engineering team processes all submissions within 2 business hours. For standard geometry, our automated slicing engine returns instant dimensional feedback, while complex multi-part assemblies receive dedicated human DFM validation within 12 hours.
All data is encrypted in transit and at rest using AES-256 protocols. We routinely execute mutual enterprise NDAs and can process defense-related projects in air-gapped, ITAR-compliant facilities with strict zero-knowledge data retention policies.
