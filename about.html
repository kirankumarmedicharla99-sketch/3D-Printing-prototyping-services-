PURPOSE & PRINCIPLES

DEFINING OUR PURPOSE.
DRIVING PRECISION.

We exist to remove the mechanical friction between imagination and reality, empowering engineering pioneers across aerospace, automotive, medical, and robotics.

OUR DRIVING FORCE

OUR MISSION

To eliminate the manufacturing bottlenecks between CAD files and functional physical parts, giving every innovator access to industrial-grade additive manufacturing without prohibitive capital tooling costs.

Micron-Level Dimensional Fidelity: Laser-scanned accuracy to ±0.02mm for seamless functional assemblies.
Radical Time Compression: Moving from validated CAD 3D models to working test prototypes within 24 to 48 hours.
Zero-Tooling Economics: No upfront mold tooling fees, allowing cost-effective batch production from 1 to 10,000 units.
OUR LONG-TERM HORIZON

OUR VISION

To establish the global benchmark for sustainable, on-demand autonomous fabrication — enabling lightweight generative designs that consume fewer materials and eliminate global warehouse stockpiling.

Generative Topology Freedom: Creating organic Voronoi and biomimetic structures that reduce component weight by 40%.
Closed-Loop Circular Polymers: 100% post-production powder reclamation and zero-waste thermoplastic reprocessing.
Decentralized Production Hubs: A global network of connected additive chambers ensuring local, low-carbon manufacturing.
CHRONOLOGY OF INNOVATION

A DECADE OF
ENGINEERING EXCELLENCE.

2016 FOUNDATION

The Inception

PrintNexa was founded in Tech Valley by a team of two mechanical engineers. Starting with dual high-temperature FDM chambers, we tackled prototype brackets that standard 3D printers could not handle.

2 Chambers PLA & ABS+ Robotics Startups
2018 CAPABILITY SCALE

SLA Resin Laboratory & First 100 Clients

Expanded into stereolithography (SLA) resin micro-printing, achieving 25-micron layer resolution. Delivered our first 100+ commercial projects and opened industrial UV post-curing facilities.

SLA Resin Suite 100+ Enterprise Clients Medical Models
2020 POWDER BED TECH

Industrial SLS & ISO Class 7 Cleanroom

Commissioned Selective Laser Sintering (SLS) systems for support-free PA12 Nylon production. Constructed an ISO Class 7 certified post-finishing cleanroom for medical and aerospace compliance.

SLS PA12 Nylon Class 7 Cleanroom Vapor Smoothing
