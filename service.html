FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

CLEAR ANSWERS TO
TECHNICAL QUESTIONS.

Need custom tolerances, material datasheets, or custom batch quotes? Everything you need to know about our production workflows.

Have specific tolerances?

Speak directly with our senior applications engineer.

engineering@printnexa.com

We accept native CAD and neutral formats including STEP (.step, .stp), STL (.stl), Parasolid (.x_t), IGES (.igs), and 3MF (.3mf). For multi-material or assembly verification, STEP and Parasolid formats provide the highest mathematical curve precision.

Our standard industrial SLA and DMLS metal lines guarantee ±0.02 mm to ±0.05 mm. Industrial Selective Laser Sintering (SLS) delivers ±0.1 mm. For critical bearing press fits or O-ring grooves, we offer secondary precision CNC milling and reaming.

Urgent FDM and SLA orders dispatched under our 24-Hour Express Protocol ship next-morning. Standard prototype runs typically take 2 to 4 business days, while certified DMLS titanium components require 3 to 5 business days including stress-relief heat treatment.

Every upload is encrypted under AES-256 and subject to strict confidentiality agreements. We routinely execute bilateral non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) with tier-1 defense, aerospace, and medical partners prior to receiving CAD geometry.

Our post-processing facility offers chemical vapor smoothing (achieving injection-molded gloss), media bead blasting, ultrasonic cleaning, Cerakote ceramic coating, custom RAL color dyeing, electroplating, and threaded brass heat-set insert installation.
